Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Thousandfold is a free browser game run by one independent developer. This page explains what the game stores about you and why.

Without an account

Your characters and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in (with email and password, or with Google), we store:

  • Your email address, to identify your account. With Google sign-in we also receive your name and profile picture from Google; the game does not display or use them.
  • Your game data: your cloud save, your characters' names and progress for the leaderboards, and the player name and friend code you choose.
  • Social activity: your friends list and blocks, chat messages, letters and gifts you send or receive, and trades. Public chat messages are kept for 14 days. Letters expire after 30 days.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers (for example, IP addresses in server logs) for security and fixing problems.

Who can see it

  • Other players can see your player name, your characters' names and leaderboard scores, and messages you post in public chat.
  • Private messages, letters and trades are visible only to the players involved, and to the game's administrator when needed to fix problems or stop abuse.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and game data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google provides “Sign in with Google” if you choose it.
  • Google Fonts provides the game's typefaces.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything linked to it, email chiduc3010@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Data in your browser can be removed by deleting your characters or clearing the site's data in your browser.

Children

Thousandfold is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
